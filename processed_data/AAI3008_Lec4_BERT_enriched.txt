We only predict the masked words rather than reconstructing the entire input.

# How SIT Internal SiT Parameter Count is Calculated in GPT

- The parameter count in GPT is mainly determined by the number of learnable weights 𝑊𝑊 and biases in the model.
- In practice, almost all parameters come from large weight matrices used in embeddings, self-attention, and feed-forward layers.
- Bias terms exist, but their contribution to the total parameter count is relatively small and often negligible compared to weights.

# Memory for Optimizer States (Training Only)

- Adam optimizer
- The first moment estimates the mean of gradients (direction)
- The second moment estimates the uncentered variance of gradients (scale)
- Adaptive and stable parameter updates

# First moment (mean of gradients)

mt = β1mt−1 + (1 − β1)gt

# Second moment (mean of squared gradients)

vt = β2vt−1 + (1 − β2)gt2

# Bias correction

vt = vt / (1 - β2)

mt = mt / (1 - β1)

# Parameter update

θt+1 = θt − α mt / (√vt + ε)
