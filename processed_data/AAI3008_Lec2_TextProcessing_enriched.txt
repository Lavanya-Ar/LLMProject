# What is Bag of Words?

A text document is represented as a vector of word counts by ignoring word order and position, while retaining only the frequency of each word.

# Discussion

- One input, multiple true output
- The training target is to get the vectors
- Not the one-to-one mapping
- Reduce the computational complexity
- Negative Sampling*
- Focuses on a small subset of "negative" samples instead of the full vocabulary
- Hierarchical Softmax*
- Reduces computational complexity by structuring the vocabulary as a binary tree
- Huffman Tree*
- Huffman tree is built by repeatedly merging the two least frequent nodes.
- Assign shorter paths to more frequent words, making training even more efficient.
